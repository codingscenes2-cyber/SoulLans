# SOUL LAND: THE FALLEN REALM — design overview

## Premise
Seven primordial powers once governed existence through seven Soul Thrones: Life, Death, Time, Flame, Void, Memory and Creation. Nine hundred years ago, the Seventh Throne was destroyed during the Great Fracture. The world broke into unstable regions; the dead returned, old powers stirred and kingdoms rewrote their histories to hide what happened.

The player bears an unexplained connection to the missing Throne. The prophecy surrounding that connection is incomplete and may be deliberately deceptive. The central mystery should unfold through exploration, item descriptions, NPC testimony, environmental clues and mutually conflicting accounts.

## Design pillars
1. Weighty third-person combat with clear attack tells and meaningful recovery.
2. Exploration that rewards curiosity with lore, shortcuts, equipment and hidden encounters.
3. Character builds that support different weapon, spell and defensive approaches.
4. NPCs and factions whose outcomes can change as the world state changes.
5. A persistent death/recovery loop that creates tension without arbitrary punishment.

## Planned regions
- Ashen Kingdom — starting region and first vertical slice.
- Hollow Forest — ancient growth, corrupted wildlife and lost paths.
- Kingdom of Velmora — political conflict, fortified settlements and faction quests.
- Frostfall Highlands — harsh traversal and frozen ruins.
- Ember Depths — volcanic caverns and flame-bound enemies.
- Sunken Catacombs — drowned burial architecture and necromantic secrets.
- Forgotten Citadel — late-game convergence of the throne mystery.

## Progression
Soul Essence supports character levelling and upgrades. Sanctuaries restore resources, allow preparation and respawn ordinary enemies. On death, unspent Soul Essence is dropped at the death location and can be recovered before another death.

## Endings
Plan for at least seven endings, each requiring persistent story flags and meaningful choices. Ending names and conditions should remain subject to narrative design review; do not expose the central truth too early.

## Scope discipline
First deliver a polished Ashen Kingdom vertical slice with one complete combat loop, one Sanctuary, one NPC quest and one boss. Only then expand the world.
